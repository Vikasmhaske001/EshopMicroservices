ESHOPMICROSERVICES · UI REDESIGN · PALETTE 3B MANGO & GRAPE · DESKTOP 1440

Four concepts, six screens each

All artboards share one live state. Filtering, sorting or searching in any concept updates all four, and so does "View" (which loads the details screen), adding to the basket, changing quantities, checkout and sign-in. The dataState tweak shows the catalogue's loading, empty and error states. Sign in with the password wrong to see the error. Columns: 1a Premium Minimal · 1b Gen Z Editorial · 1c Modern Marketplace · 1d Refined Signature (recommended).

Stack. Angular 21 standalone components and signals, with Angular Material 21 (M3 azure theme), lazy routes, an auth guard, and Playwright e2e tests.
Why images are missing. Catalog.Api stores only an imageFile name (product-1…6.png), and no static-file endpoint serves it. The card shows a placeholder on purpose. The fix belongs in the backend.
Fallback. Illustrated tiles are mapped from the product category, and each tile shows its imageFile name. No image URLs are invented.
Data issue. The seed data puts Huawei and Xiaomi under "White Appliances" and the LG phone under "Home Kitchen". product-6.png is also shared by two products. The tiles follow the category as given.
Supported, so shown. Category, search, name/price sort, 9-per-page pagination, a by-category endpoint ("More in…"), basket qty/remove/clear, async order polling, and returnUrl login.
Not supported, so not shown. Shipping cost, ratings, stock, wishlist, compare, price filter. The discount row appears only when discountAmount > 0.
